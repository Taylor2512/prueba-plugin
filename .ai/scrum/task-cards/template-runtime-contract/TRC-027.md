# TRC-027 — Validate activeUserId and activeDocumentId states

## Objective

Fail closed on invalid active references with explicit readiness states.

## Required states

NO_USERS, NO_DOCUMENTS, INVALID_ACTIVE_USER, INVALID_ACTIVE_DOCUMENT, READY

## Acceptance

- runtime init and restore reject unknown active IDs deterministically;
- policy for fallback (if any) is explicit and test-backed;
- ownership-sensitive flows do not silently pick first element.

## Safety

No implicit fallback that changes ownership semantics.
