# PRT-070 — BrowserContext multi-user and multi-document isolation

## Objective

Use separate browser contexts and rapid User/Document switching to prove values, dirty
state, remote caches and artifacts remain isolated.

## Product boundary

SISAD-PDFME reusable. No concrete consumer business semantics.

## Acceptance

- two independent users;
- multiple documents;
- same-realm Forms plus separate BrowserContexts;
- stale responses rejected;
- no cross-scope leakage.

1. reconcile live source/evidence/task dependencies;
2. characterize first divergence;
3. implement the smallest correct authority;
4. execute focal/integration/browser gates appropriate to risk;
5. write evidence;
6. update status only from executed facts.
